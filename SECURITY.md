# Security Policy & Compliance Dossier

## 1. Protected Health Information (PHI) & HIPAA Compliance
PERSEPHONE is currently a **clinical research prototype and simulation engine**. 
- **No Patient Health Information (PHI):** The system does not ingest, store, or transmit identifiable patient records.
- **Synthetic Data Baseline:** All patient profiles (Elena Rostova, Arthur Pendelton, Marcus Vance) and their associated genomics, clinical notes, and biometrics are entirely **synthetic and simulated** for demonstration and research benchmarking.

## 2. Research Prototype Disclaimer
> [!WARNING]
> PERSEPHONE is designed exclusively for computational oncology simulation and decision-support research. It is **not approved for diagnostic use, clinical practice, or active patient care**. All drug dosing modifications and simulation projections must be verified by a board-certified oncologist using validated diagnostic tools.

## 3. Reporting Vulnerabilities
If you discover a security vulnerability or security gap in the simulation architecture, please do not open a public issue. Instead, report it privately to the development team at `dev.mehta@persephone-oncology.org`.
