# PERSEPHONE Workstation Walkthrough & Demo Script

This script provides reviewers and judges with a step-by-step narrative to explore the key clinical features of the PERSEPHONE workstation.

---

## Demo 1: Patient A (BRCA1-mutant Ovarian Cancer)
- **Objective:** Demonstrate synthetic lethality and clonal selection modeling.
- **Narrative Steps:**
  1. **Select Patient:** In the top header panel, click on **Elena Rostova** (Patient A).
  2. **Observe Digital Twin:** The sidebar will populate her clinical profile, displaying diagnostic staging (`Stage IIIC HGSOC`) and active somatic mutations (pathogenic `BRCA1 c.1961delA` frameshift mutation at `42.3% VAF`).
  3. **Inspect Telemetry:** The heart rate and temperature charts show live, fluctuating signals generated by the stochastic IoT stream.
  4. **Examine Histopathology:** Verify that the slide viewer shows the histopathology slide image loaded via the secure server proxy endpoint.

---

## Demo 2: Patient B (EGFR-mutant NSCLC with Acquired Resistance)
- **Objective:** Analyze bypass pathway activation and drug resistance selection.
- **Narrative Steps:**
  1. **Select Patient:** Click on **Arthur Pendelton** (Patient B).
  2. **Review Genomics:** Observe the co-existence of activating `EGFR L858R` (VAF 48.9%) and acquired gatekeeper resistance `EGFR T790M` (VAF 18.5%).
  3. **Observe Clinical State:** Arthur is marked as `Progressive Disease` on 1st-generation EGFR TKIs (Erlotinib) due to T790M's steric ATP affinity alterations.

## Demo 3: Adaptive Dosing vs. Continuous MTD Simulation
- **Objective:** Mathematically demonstrate Gatenby-style competitive suppression.
- **Narrative Steps:**
  1. **Open Simulation Lab:** Locate the simulation control panel.
  2. **Factual Simulation (MTD):** Select the MTD strategy. Observe the simulation curve: the sensitive cells collapse immediately, but the resistant cells expand rapidly, leading to progression (TTP ~47 days).
  3. **Counterfactual Engine:** Switch the comparison strategy slider to **Adaptive**.
  4. **Analyze Curves:** Factual projections (solid lines) are overlaid with counterfactual projections (dashed lines). Notice the Adaptive regimen holds dosing when volume drops, allowing sensitive clones to suppress resistant expansion. Time-to-Progression (TTP) shifts to **137.5 days**—a massive extension.
  5. **Review Deltas:** The comparison metrics scorecard calculates the exact percent drug dose saved and toxicity reduction.

## Demo 4: Knowledge Graph Exploration
- **Objective:** Trace causal biological pathways.
- **Narrative Steps:**
  1. **Navigate to Graph Explorer:** Locate the interactive visual canvas.
  2. **Observe Force Simulation:** Nodes represent patients, mutations, genes, pathways, drugs, and clinical trials. Drag nodes to inspect attraction/repulsion.
  3. **Trace Pathway:** Hover over Arthur Pendelton. Observe the highlighted paths trace:
     `Arthur Pendelton` $\to$ `MET amp` $\to$ `MET` $\to$ `met-pathway` $\to$ `savolitinib` $\to$ `NCT03944772` (Clinical Trial).
  4. **Identify Bypass Edge:** Spot the bypass resistance mechanism connecting MET amplification to third-generation EGFR TKI resistance.

---

## Demo 5: Multi-Agent Tumor Board DAG Debate
- **Objective:** Trigger the stateful cognitive pipeline.
- **Narrative Steps:**
  1. **Locate Tumor Board Terminal:** Scroll to the typewriter console panel.
  2. **Click "Re-run Agent Board":** Triggers the stateful execution sequence.
  3. **Watch DAG Progression:** The DAG nodes at the top pulse sequentially:
     - `EVOLUTION` analyzes growth rates and progression risks.
     - `PLANNING` compares dosing protocols and recommends adaptive timing.
     - `EVIDENCE` crawls references and matches clinical trials (e.g. savolitinib/osimertinib combinations).
     - `SAFETY` checks toxicity and renal clearances.
     - `CONSENSUS` computes the final recommendation and compiles the scorecard.
  4. **Inspect Consensus Output:** Read the typewriter-generated output text detailing the **Evidence Strength Score** and its component breakdown.
