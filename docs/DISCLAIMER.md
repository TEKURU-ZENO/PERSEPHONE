# Scientific Disclaimer & Regulatory Notice

## 1. Research Prototype Boundaries
PERSEPHONE (Predictive Evolutionary Reasoning System for Explainable Precision Healthcare & Oncology Network Engine) is an **exploratory computational oncology prototype** and decision-support modeling engine. It is designed exclusively for academic research, biomedical validation, and technological benchmarking.

> [!WARNING]
> PERSEPHONE is **not intended for clinical diagnosis, active patient treatment management, or therapeutic guidance**. It is **not approved by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** or any global regulatory body for clinical practice.

---

## 2. Mathematical Modeling & Simulation Scope
The Runge-Kutta 4th order (RK4) ODE numerical solver and Lotka-Volterra equations represent a highly abstracted mathematical model of tumor clonal competition. While calibrated against clinical trial metadata:
- The simulations do **not** account for all microenvironmental interactions, vascularization variations, immune checkpoint dynamics, or individual metabolic differences.
- Projections should be interpreted as **theoretical trends** rather than absolute clinical forecasts.
- Dosing and toxicity parameters must **never** be used to prescribe or adjust active chemotherapy or targeted therapy regimens.
