# Scientific Disclaimer & Regulatory Notice

## 1. Research Prototype Boundaries
PERSEPHONE (Predictive Evolutionary Reasoning System for Explainable Precision Healthcare & Oncology Network Engine) is an **exploratory computational oncology decision-support prototype and simulation platform** developed across 20 research phases. It is designed exclusively for academic research, technological benchmarking, and algorithmic validation.

> [!WARNING]
> PERSEPHONE is **not intended for clinical diagnosis, active patient treatment management, drug prescription, or therapeutic guidance**. It is **not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)**, the European Medicines Agency (EMA), or any regulatory authority for clinical practice.

---

## 2. Mathematical Modeling & Simulation Scope
The Runge-Kutta 4th-order (RK4) ODE numerical solver, Lotka-Volterra competition dynamics, Reinforcement Learning policies, and Synthetic Cohort simulators represent mathematical abstractions of tumor clonal competition:
- Numerical simulations do **not** account for all patient-specific microenvironmental factors, immune exhaustion pathways, three-dimensional vascularization heterogeneity, or individual pharmacokinetic variability.
- Simulation trajectories must be interpreted as **theoretical computational trends** rather than absolute medical prognoses.
- Drug dosing schedules, holidays, and toxicity curves must **never** be used to prescribe or adjust chemotherapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy in human patients.

---

## 3. Synthetic and De-identified Data Baseline
To ensure compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and international privacy frameworks:
- All baseline patient twin files (Elena Rostova, Arthur Pendelton, Marcus Vance), longitudinal monitoring records, synthetic cohorts (Phase 17), and multimodal imaging files are entirely **synthetic and simulated**.
- Any resemblance to actual patients, living or deceased, is purely coincidental.
- No real Protected Health Information (PHI) is processed, stored, or transmitted by this software.

---

## 4. Multi-Agent Governance & Clinical Abstention
The 23-agent collaborative council operates via deterministic algorithmic rules and guidelines (KDIGO 2024, CTCAE v5.0, CPIC). Where guidelines conflict, data discordance occurs, or safety boundaries are exceeded, the system triggers formal clinical abstention (`ABSTAIN`). These governance decisions reflect mathematical risk boundaries and must not replace human clinical judgment.
